ANGEL is a fragmented figural sculpture. The torso is broken into sharp geometric planes, the body is interrupted at the waist and the arms end in clean cuts. The head is reduced to a faceted form without facial detail.

The composition is asymmetric: a single wing, built from prism-like feathers, rises from one shoulder. The figure stands on a slender leg growing out of a rectangular base. Seen from the side and in profile, the relation between wing and body changes, so the work reads fully in the round.

The form is 3D printed in PLA and finished by hand with acrylics to the appearance of stone: a dark grey tone with lighter accents on the edges brings out the facets and the surface texture. Because each copy is finished by hand, small details of the surface differ between copies. The stone appearance comes from the surface finish; the sculpture is not carved stone.

Each copy is marked with its number and signed by the artist. For indoor display.
